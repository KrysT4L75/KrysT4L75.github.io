01Manuscript submitted

Learning from multi-omics
in adolescent mental health

I led data preparation, feature selection, and the development of a stacking ensemble integrating multi-omics and clinical blood data for adolescent non-suicidal self-injury (NSSI) identification.

My contribution
I compared modality-specific models, conducted ablation analyses, and led manuscript preparation as a co-first author. The work examines peripheral signatures associated with NSSI.
